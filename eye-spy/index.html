Eye Spy

Everything your browser hands over to any website, no permission prompts needed. Each value below came from a script on this page.

This page has no backend. The only thing it stores is a visit counter in your browser's local storage, to show how sites recognize you on return. A few lookups go out: your IP is sent to ipapi.co for geolocation and ipify.org for your IPv6 address, your request headers are echoed back by httpbin.org, and a WebRTC request goes to Google's STUN server to discover your network addresses. Everything else runs locally.
